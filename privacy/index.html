Privacy policy

nim.ai. In effect from 8 November 2026.

The short version. We don't collect your personal data. No accounts, no analytics, no ads, no trackers, no cookies. Nothing is sold or shared, because we don't have it.

Who we are

nim stands for Not In My. nim.ai makes tools that dial back how much machines get into your life. "We" on this page means nim.ai. Write to hello@nim.ai about anything here.

This website

nim.ai is a set of static pages. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads nothing from anyone else: every file comes from nim.ai itself.

GitHub Pages hosts the site. Like any web host, GitHub receives the basics of each request (your IP address, your browser's user agent, the page asked for and when) so it can serve the page and keep the service secure. GitHub handles that under its own privacy statement. We don't get those logs and don't ask for them.

Our tools

Each tool has its own policy page that names exactly what it reads and what it keeps. Every one of them holds to these:

  • It does its work on your device wherever it can, and says plainly where it can't.
  • No account is needed to use it.
  • No analytics, telemetry or crash reports.
  • Whatever it keeps stays on your device, and you can erase it from inside the tool.
  • Nothing is sold, rented or shared for advertising.

When you write to us

If you email us, we use your address and message only to answer you. We don't add you to a list, and we delete the thread once it's settled unless you ask us to keep it. Mail to nim.ai passes through Cloudflare's email routing on its way to our inbox.

Children

We collect nothing from anyone, children under 13 included.

Your rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR, the UK GDPR and the CCPA give you the right to see, correct and delete the personal data a company holds about you, and to object to its use. From this site and our tools we hold none. If you've written to us, ask and we'll send you what we have or delete it. We don't sell or share personal information as the CCPA defines those words.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will show the new date, and the old version stays in the site's public history on GitHub. If a tool ever changes what it handles, it will say so inside the tool and on its policy page before the change takes effect.
