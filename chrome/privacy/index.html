nim for chrome: privacy policy

In effect from 8 November 2026. Covers the Chrome extension, version 1.1.0 and later. nim.ai's policy covers everything else.

The short version. nim for chrome reads the posts on the pages you visit and decides on your computer whether each reads as AI slop. It sends nothing anywhere: no account, no analytics, no crash reports, no update checks of its own.

What it reads

The text of posts, comments and replies on the pages you visit. The extension scores that text in your browser, with its rule pack and a small style model that ship inside it, then drops it. Page text never leaves your computer. The extension makes no network requests at all, and its content security policy lets its pages load their own files and nothing else.

It stays off mail, banking and health sites and off local network addresses, and never reads those pages.

What it keeps, on your computer only

  • Your settings.
  • Your corrections. "Not slop" and "Hide as slop" keep a hash of the post, not its words, and clear themselves 90 days after you last changed them.
  • Sites you paused or set to hide completely, stored by name.
  • Authors you chose to always show, stored by name for that site.
  • The rule pack, and one count of what it has hidden since install.

All of it lives in the extension's own storage. None of it syncs to your Google account or anywhere else. Private windows write nothing to disk.

Erasing it

Open the extension's options, then Privacy, then Forget everything. That clears corrections, paused sites, authors and the count, and leaves your settings and the rule pack. Removing the extension erases all of it.

What it shares

Nothing. The data isn't sold or transferred to anyone, isn't used for anything beyond deciding what to hide or label, and isn't used to judge creditworthiness or for lending.

Updates

Chrome updates the extension from the Chrome Web Store the way it updates every extension. Google runs that under its own privacy policy. The extension itself never checks for updates or phones home.

Changes

If nim for chrome ever changes hands, these promises go with it. Any change to what it handles will be announced in the store listing and in the extension before it takes effect, and this page will show the new date.
