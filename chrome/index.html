nim for chrome

AI? Not In My chrome. In beta.

A Chrome extension that hides the obvious AI slop in posts, comments and replies, and flags the less obvious. It goes by how the text reads, not who wrote it, and everything runs on your computer.

  • Hides the obvious, like citation debris pasted out of a chat tool, behind one line you can open.
  • Also hides a post when strong signs and a small style model on your computer agree.
  • Flags the rest with a label or a quiet mark, and shows why.
  • Takes corrections, and lets you pause any site.

It sends nothing anywhere. No account, no analytics, no crash reports, no update checks of its own. Privacy policy.
