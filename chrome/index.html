AI? Not In My chrome. An experimental extension, in beta.

Fair warning first: this is an experiment. It'll get things wrong, and how it decides will keep changing while we tune it.

It reads the posts, comments and replies on pages you visit and tucks away the obvious AI slop, the kind with chatbot citation junk still stuck to it. Subtler stuff gets a label or a small mark instead. Hover the mark and it shows you why.

It judges how the text reads, not who wrote it. Plenty of people write like a press release, so it'll flag some of them. One click puts a post back, and it remembers. You can also switch it off for any site.

It all runs on your computer. The model ships inside the extension, so there's nothing to send and it sends nothing. There's no account to make and no analytics. The privacy policy says the same thing in more words.

Got a post it flagged wrong, or one it missed? hello@nim.ai
